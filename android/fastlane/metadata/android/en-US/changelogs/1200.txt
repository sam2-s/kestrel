A share now survives the app being closed. Sharing still stops when you swipe
the app away, because the keys that seal each position go with the process, but
the app writes down that a share was running and puts it back when you reopen
it. A share you stopped yourself stays stopped, a timed share that ran out stays
ended, and a locked phone resumes nothing until you unlock it.

Two members changing the circle's keys at the same moment no longer splits the
circle across two channels without telling anyone. The side that loses the tie
rejoins the other. A re-key that removes or admits someone is never reconciled
this way, so nobody gets added back by their own removal.
