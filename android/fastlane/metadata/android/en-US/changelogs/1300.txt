New switch in Settings, under Sharing: keep sharing when the app is closed. Off
by default. With it on, swiping Starling out of recents no longer stops a share.
Nothing about the encryption changed, and no position or key is handed to the
wrapper; the page that does the encrypting is simply kept running until the
share ends.

It costs something, and the setting says so: a phone you believe you closed is
still running Starling and still holding your circle's keys, which the app lock
cannot protect until the share ends. When the share does end, the page goes with
it a few seconds later.

A timed share now ends on the next position rather than on a countdown the
system is free to delay, so a closed app cannot overrun the window you set.
